Spectral Explorer

Spectral Explorer allows you to investigate specific regions of the sky by entering their coordinates and selecting an astronomical image for analysis. The tool helps you explore the spectral information associated with each observation, including radiation signatures, absorption lines, and frequency variations. By examining these characteristics, you can better understand how light interacts with celestial objects and discover valuable information hidden within astronomical observations.
