About the Project

What is Space Collectors?

We are a group of students passionate about space science and interested in developing new learning tools for people of all backgrounds. We aim to create a project that helps address challenges in space exploration and makes complex information more accessible.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a web-based tool that makes it easier to understand data collected by SPHEREx, while offering an interactive learning experience where users can explore information about astronomical images and discover how they change over time.
